Frequently asked questions

Which countries do you work with?

Clients anywhere in the world. Most of our clients are in the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe and Africa, and we welcome projects from every region.

Where are your developers based?

Everywhere. Our developers, AI engineers and security experts work remotely from across the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia, so your team can cover your working hours.

How quickly can I get a team?

You can post a job in minutes. Our AI assistant turns your idea into a clear plan, you see matched developers straight away, and you can add them to your team or let them join from the job board.

How does pricing work?

Senior developers start at $45 an hour with no minimum, and you only pay for work you approve. Site care is $249 a month, a full-time dedicated developer starts at $5,600 a month, and product teams start at $15,200 a month. There are no long-term contracts, the first two weeks are a risk-free trial, and there is no fee to hire your developer directly.

Can I hire for AI projects?

Yes. Our AI engineers build chatbots, document search, automations and features powered by large language models, and can add AI to a product you already have.

How do you vet your developers?

Every developer, AI engineer and security expert is vetted before they can join a team. We check their experience and past work, and they go through rigorous interviews: a technical interview on their skills, a live problem-solving session and a communication interview in English. Only those who pass every stage are accepted.

I'm a developer. Can I join from any country?

Yes. Create a free developer profile, pass our vetting and interviews, then set your own rate and availability and join client teams that match your skills, wherever you live.
