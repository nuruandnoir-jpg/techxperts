Full-stack developer hiring FAQ

What is a full-stack developer?

A full-stack developer builds both sides of a web product: the front end people see and click (pages, forms, dashboards) and the back end that powers it (servers, APIs, databases, payments and logins). One full-stack developer can take a feature from idea to launch on their own.

How much does it cost to hire a full-stack developer?

A senior full-stack developer costs $45 an hour, or $7,200 a month full-time. Mid-level full-stack developers are $35 an hour ($5,600 a month), and expert or lead developers are $63 an hour ($10,080 a month). There are no long-term contracts, and you only pay for work you approve.

Should I hire a full-stack developer or specialists?

Hire a full-stack developer when you need one person to own a product or feature end to end, especially for an MVP, a web app or a website. Add specialists, such as a UI/UX designer, a mobile developer, a cloud engineer or a QA tester, as the product and the team grow. We can do both.

How are your full-stack developers vetted?

Every developer is vetted and passes rigorous interviews before joining a team: a review of products they have shipped, a technical interview across front end, back end and databases, a live coding session, and a communication interview in English.

Which technologies do your developers use?

Mostly JavaScript and TypeScript with React, Next.js, Vue and Node.js, plus Python with Django or FastAPI, PHP with Laravel, PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB, and cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, Vercel and Cloudflare. Tell us your stack and we will match it.

Can a full-stack developer work on my existing website or app?

Yes. Many clients bring an existing site or app that needs new features, faster pages, bug fixes, a redesign or a rescue. Your developer starts by reviewing the code and gives you a clear plan before changing anything.

Do you integrate local payment methods?

Yes. Our developers integrate Stripe, PayPal and Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as payment providers popular in Africa and elsewhere, such as M-Pesa, Paystack and Flutterwave.

Which countries do you work with?

Clients everywhere, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, Asia Pacific and Latin America. Our full-stack developers work remotely across the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia, so your team can cover your working hours.
