AI engineer hiring FAQ

What does an AI engineer do?

An AI engineer builds features powered by artificial intelligence: chatbots and assistants, search over your documents, automations that read and write for you, and models that predict or classify. They connect large language models such as Claude and GPT to your data and your product, and make the results reliable, safe and affordable to run.

How much does it cost to hire an AI engineer?

A senior AI engineer costs $60 an hour, or $9,600 a month full-time. Mid-level AI engineers start at $47 an hour ($7,520 a month), and expert or lead AI engineers are $84 an hour ($13,440 a month). There are no long-term contracts, and you only pay for work you approve.

How are your AI engineers vetted?

Every AI engineer is vetted and passes rigorous interviews before joining a team: a review of their past AI work, a technical interview on machine learning and large language models, a live build of a small AI feature, and a communication interview in English.

How fast can an AI engineer start?

Usually within days. Post your project, our AI assistant turns it into a clear plan, and you see matched AI engineers straight away. You can add them to your team yourself or let them join from the job board.

Can you add AI to the product I already have?

Yes. Most of our AI work is adding a feature to an existing website, app or internal tool: a support chatbot, smart search, document processing or an automation. We work with your current stack.

Is my data safe?

Your engineer works inside the accounts and tools you give them access to, follows your security rules, and can use providers and settings that keep your data out of model training. Ask for an NDA at any time.

Do you only hire individual AI engineers?

No. You can hire one AI engineer, or a complete AI team, for example an AI engineer, a full-stack developer and a QA tester working together to ship your feature.

Which countries do you work with?

Clients everywhere, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, Asia Pacific and Latin America. Our AI engineers work remotely across the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia, so your team can cover your working hours.
